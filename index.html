Comfort & Mobility First

Getting to Steele Wheels & Accessibility

Steele Wheels Motor Museum · 66 Otter Lake Court, Halifax, NS (Bayers Lake Business Park)

Accessibility & On-Site Comfort

  • ✓
    100% Wheelchair & Walker Accessible: Flat ground-floor entry, wide 6-ft+ aisles, and full elevator access to the upper Mezzanine booths.
  • ✓
    Loaner Wheelchairs & Rest Seating: On-site loaner wheelchairs at the Welcome Desk plus cushioned rest zones every 50 feet.
  • ✓
    Giant LED Bingo Display & Clear Acoustics: Every Bingo call appears huge on the stage LED wall, while Mezzanine resource booths offer a calmer space to chat.
  • ✓
    Full Daylight Departure at 7:00 PM: Sunset in Halifax on May 8 isn’t until ~8:25 PM, so guests staying for the 6 PM Finale Bingo still travel home in bright daylight!

Parking, Drop-Off & Group Buses

  • ✓
    Free On-Site Parking: Plenty of complimentary parking on-site (primary parking lot is directly behind the building, with accessible spots up front).
  • ✓
    Front-Door Passenger Drop-Off Lane: Pull right up to the front doors to drop off passengers on flat pavement before parking.
  • ✓
    Access-A-Bus & Retirement Buses Welcome: Registered Access-A-Bus riders can book directly to 66 Otter Lake Court (remember to book up to 7 days ahead!).
  • ✓
    Transit & Shuttle Note: Because regular Halifax Transit buses stop at Lacewood Terminal / Chain Lake Dr rather than right on Otter Lake Ct, check the box on the RSVP form if you’d like updates on our Lacewood Terminal Shuttle Loop!
