Saturday, May 8, 2027 · 11:00 AM – 7:00 PM · Steele Wheels Motor Museum, Halifax

Classic cars, live music, and four hours of free bingo.

A free Saturday gathering for older adults, families, and friends inside Bayers Lake’s 40,000 sq. ft. motor museum. Drop in for a couple of hours or spend the day moving between live stage sets, sixty-minute prize bingos, and 75 vintage vehicles.

The Main Showroom

A live performance every odd hour. Prize bingo every even hour.

We paced the day so nobody is stuck standing—or sitting—too long. Watch or dance to swing at 11 AM, take a seat at noon for sixty minutes of free prize bingo, catch the 1 PM live band, play again at 2 PM, settle in for the 3 PM stage show, play the 4 PM round, enjoy live vocals at 5 PM, and wrap with our 6 PM finale bingo.

  • Strict 60-minute bingo clock Starts & finishes on the dot
  • Easy-read slider cards & LED wall Every call shown huge on stage
  • Gift cards & local baskets Four games inside every bingo hour
The Mezzanine & Airstream

Coffee from a vintage Airstream. Quiet booths upstairs.

Down on the showroom floor, Steele Wheels’ polished stainless-steel Airstream café pours fresh coffee, decaf, and tea alongside lunch and supper bites, surrounded by 75+ classic cars and a vintage convertible photo spot.

Take the elevator up to the bright mezzanine overlooking the cars to visit 30+ local health, recreation, and community tables—intentionally separated from main-stage volume so you can actually hear the person across the table.

  • Showroom collection 75+ classic cars & motorcycles
  • Community & wellness expo Upper mezzanine via elevator
  • Food & refreshments Coffee, lunch & supper service

Plenty of tables and seating surround the Airstream lounge and main showroom floor.

Getting Here

Practical details for the day.

Steele Wheels Motor Museum
66 Otter Lake Court, Halifax, NS
01

Parking & front drop-off

Parking is free on-site, with the main lot right behind the building and accessible stalls out front. A flat front-door lane stays open all day for passenger and bus drop-offs.

02

Wheelchairs & elevator

The entire ground showroom is step-free with wide aisles, an elevator serves the upper mezzanine booths, and loaner wheelchairs are available at the welcome desk.

03

Transit & group buses

Access-A-Bus and retirement residence buses can pull right to the front doors. Since regular buses stop at Lacewood Terminal rather than Otter Lake Ct, check the shuttle box when you RSVP for shuttle updates.

04

Home in full daylight

Sunset in Halifax on May 8 isn’t until around 8:25 PM—so even if you stay right through the 6:00 PM finale bingo, you’ll still head home in bright daylight.
