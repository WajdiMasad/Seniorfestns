Community Sponsorship Invitation: Forever Young, Seniors’ Celebration

Dear [First Name],

On Saturday, May 8, 2027, hundreds of older adults, retirees, and family caregivers from across the Halifax Regional Municipality will gather inside Steele Wheels Motor Museum in Bayers Lake for Forever Young: Seniors’ Celebration.

Every guest enters free of charge, parks free of charge, and plays four rounds of prize bingo free of charge. We are inviting a select circle of trusted Halifax businesses to partner with us in bringing this celebration to life while building lasting, face-to-face relationships with local seniors and their families.

8 hours live 11 am to 7 pm across two natural attendance waves
4 stage acts Swing dancing, 50s–70s band, stage show & live vocals
4 free bingos 60-minute prize sessions at 12, 2, 4 & 6 pm on the LED wall
40,000 sq. ft. 75+ classic cars downstairs & quiet expo upstairs

Unlike a standard folding-table trade show, Forever Young paces the day so attendees stay engaged for hours: live music and stage shows run on the odd hours, seated 60-minute prize bingos run on the even hours, coffee and meals are served from Steele Wheels’ indoor silver Airstream diner, and our elevator-accessible Mezzanine Expo gives sponsors a calm, comfortable setting to speak directly with guests away from stage audio.

Whether you are looking for headline brand visibility as our Platinum ($5,000), Gold ($2,500), or Silver ($1,500) partner, a direct exhibitor presence as a Community Partner ($500), or hands-on goodwill as our Bingo Prize Sponsor, Goodie Bags Sponsor, or In-Kind Partner, the attached sheet outlines each option clearly.

Because showroom and mezzanine booth spaces are capped to keep aisles wide and wheelchair-accessible, partnerships are confirmed in the order received. Please reach out directly at [Phone Number] or [Email Address] so we can reserve the best fit for [Business Name].
